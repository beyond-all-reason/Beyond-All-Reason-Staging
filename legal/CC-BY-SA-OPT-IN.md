# CC-BY-SA 4.0 Opt-In — Beyond All Reason

SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-SA-4.0

If you prefer your art or audio contribution to be available for open community reuse,
you may opt in to the **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International** license
instead of the default BAR Project License (CLA).

## What this means

- Anyone may reuse, remix, and redistribute your work — with attribution and under the same license.
- You still retain copyright.
- BAR may use your work on the same terms as everyone else.

## How to opt in

During the pull request process, when prompted by the CLA bot, comment:

```
I opt for CC-BY-SA-4.0 for the art assets in this PR.
```

You may also include `SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-SA-4.0` in your commit message
or as the first comment line in text-based asset files.

## Full license text

The full CC-BY-SA 4.0 license text is included in this repository at:
[`LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`](../LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt)

The canonical version is also available at:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode

## Note

If your assets are already committed with CC-BY-SA 4.0, no action is required.
